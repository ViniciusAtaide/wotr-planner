## 2. The concept

Shared: `<key> -> <decision>` lines; line numbers cited by errors and stale reports; a two-sided reconciliation (an unlisted item, or a line matching no item) in three of four; every decision names an owning ticket except Narrowings (a prose reason).

Differences in substance:
- **Key shape:** Known and Facts match a hierarchy of patterns by specificity; Carriers and Narrowings match exact keys that are writer-produced strings (`audit` hit text; `Narrowing.said` from `Named`, `prerequisites.rs:458-501`).
- **Direction:** Known and Carriers defer a known difference (check passes; the ticket will remove it); Narrowings grants permission to write; Facts is three-valued: Free (no line), Held (writer refuses), Applied(ticket) (writer writes and logs).
- **Missing line fails?** Known, Carriers yes; Facts no (no line = Free); Narrowings only indirectly (the blocked narrowing becomes a finding `known.txt` must list).
- **Stale line fails?** Known, Carriers, Narrowings yes; Facts only for an entry missing from the data — a `held` line whose difference has gone stays forever.
- **Regenerates?** Only Carriers, keeping only the header (`carriers_known.rs:110-124`). `known.txt` keeps prose comment blocks between lines (blueprints, tickets: `known.txt:15-60`) that such a regeneration would delete.

## 7. Shapes (uncommitted)

- **A. A shared reconciliation core only:** `reconcile(lines, items, matches) -> { matched, unlisted, stale }` plus one line reader (numbers, `#` rule, ` -> ` split, duplicate refusal, the file's name in errors); adapters keep grammars and decision types. Real but modest depth, low risk, all tests survive. Facts doesn't fit unless its stale rule becomes "no change matches".
- **B. A full `Ledger<Key, Decision>`:** one grammar, a `Key` trait (exact or by specificity), a `Decision` trait, one stale rule, comment-keeping regeneration, one `Report::with_ledger`. Needs two matching strategies, a three-valued Decision for Facts, Narrowings' inherited reasons. For Facts and Narrowings the deletion test says it mostly moves complexity into generics; regeneration risks spurious hits on guarded `held` lines. Parser tests survive; semantic tests stay in adapters.
- **C. Split by direction.** Check side ("known differences"): carrier hits become findings of a new `Area` (kind `holds`/`left whole`), so `carriers-known.txt` folds into `known.txt`'s grammar; `carriers_known.rs`, `with_carriers`, `with_stale_narrowings` collapse into `Report::build`'s one path; 307c's `carriers-left.txt` becomes one more area, not a fifth parser; a `--known-format` for one area can regenerate its block between comment markers. Writer side ("write registry"): Facts plus Narrowings decide Free, Held, Applied or Allowed for a change. Concentrates the most behaviour, leaves `user-facts.txt` untouched. Cost: carrier tests change interface (through `check`); a hit's free-text key becomes Finding fields. Ask first whether the empty narrowing allowlist still earns its keep — deleting it removes one `plan` per check.

Surviving tests in every shape: parser refusals, embedded files parse, most specific line wins, stale lines fail the check (rewritten against the shared interface).
