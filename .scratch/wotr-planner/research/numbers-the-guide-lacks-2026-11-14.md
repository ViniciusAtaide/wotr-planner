## Online research: rules and items (2026-11-14)

By an agent, from the game's 2.7.0x blueprint dump (`factubsio/BubblePrintsData`, release `2.7.0x_0`) and its decompiled code (`xADDBx/WrathDecompiled`), cross-checked against mod code. - **D1:** Greater Power Attack with Power Attack (Mythic) gives +6 damage a step. Greater Power Attack checks the grip, so a one-handed weapon held in two hands counts.
- **D2:** Demonic Rage stacks with a barbarian's Rage. It is untyped: +2, +3 at MR 6, +4 at MR 9, and it gives Tireless Rage. A use lasts to the end of combat; 3 a day, +1 at MR 6 and 9.
- **D3:** one major aspect active at a time. Minor aspects: 1, then 2 from MR 5 and 3 from MR 8. [medium: the data lists the major-aspect pick twice at MR 5]
- **D4:** a natural 1 counted as 20 hits and threatens a critical, for the whole party.
- **D5:** yes, after a first melee hit on a shaken or frightened foe: later melee attacks within about a round treat it as flat-footed, so any sneak attack applies (Aspect of Babau's dice in Demonic Rage included).
- **D6:** the three +1 multipliers stack: a falchion goes from ×2 to ×5.
- **D7:** Fencer's Gift adds 2 ranks to every Weapon Training feature, Two-Handed Weapon Training included.
- **D8:** Keen and Improved Critical don't stack. The Trickster's +1 range feats add after the doubling.
- **E1:** Boots of the Battle Herald: +4 morale to attack, saves and skills, 11 temporary HP (caster level 11) and immunity to fear.

## Aivu's attack dice, from the blueprints and the decompiled code (2026-11-14)

The user: "you can use the code to check rules". Read from `xADDBx/WrathDecompiled`: `ItemEntityWeapon.Size`, `BlueprintItemWeapon.BaseDamage` and `ScaleDamage`, `RuleCalculateWeaponStats` and `RuleCalculateWeaponSizeBonus`, `WeaponDamageScaleTable`.

**The rule.**
- A weapon's dice start from its type's base dice, unless the item overrides them.
- For a unit of the player's faction (Aivu is one), a weapon's size is the wielder's size.
- The dice are scaled from Medium to that size by the game's scale table.

**Her weapons** (`AzataDragonUnit`'s body):
- bite: primary hand, `BiteLarge2d6`, no override, so the Bite type's 1d6;
- two claws: secondary hand and an extra limb, `ClawLarge1d8`, overridden to 1d8;
- two wings: secondary limbs, `WingLarge1d6`, the Wing type's 1d4;
- tail: secondary limb, `TailLarge1d8`, the Tail type's 1d6.

The "Large" in the item names is not what the game rolls for her.

| Size (MR) | Bite | Claws ×2 | Wings ×2 | Tail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small (MR 3–4) | 1d4 | 1d6 | 1d3 | 1d4 |
| Medium (MR 5–6) | 1d6 | 1d8 | 1d4 | 1d6 |
| Large (MR 7–10) | 1d8 | 2d6 | 1d6 | 1d8 |

The bite and claws are primary; the wings and tail are secondary, from the body's limb slots. This differs from the user's rule for animal companions (C11), which makes claws secondary. Aivu's body says which is which.

**Caveat.** The item tooltip (`ItemEntityWeapon.DamageDice`) scales an overridden weapon from its own blueprint size. The attack rule (`RuleCalculateWeaponSizeBonus`) scales from Medium. So an in-game tooltip may show other claw dice than the roll. The table follows the rule.
