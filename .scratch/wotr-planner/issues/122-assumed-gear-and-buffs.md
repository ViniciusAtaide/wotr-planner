# Assumed gear and buffs for the numbers

Type: design
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none (the scope decision was taken by the recommendation, option B, while the user slept, 2026-11-09). The guide's item pages are saved (2026-11-09).
Map: [WotR Build Planner](../map.md)

## Question

The map's Out of scope keeps gear out of a Build, gear bonuses toward prerequisites included (user, 2026-10-31). That rule is about legality, and it stays. But the numbers the planner now computes ([114](114-attack-line-and-damage.md)'s attack line, [119](119-caster-line.md)'s caster line if built) are always without gear, so they understate a level 20 character by a lot:
- The Max DC run (2026-11-09) ended at Charisma 25. The agent guessed "a +6 headband and inherent bonuses" add 3–5 DC, from memory. The saved item pages show the guide lists +8 headbands, so the guess was low.
- The same holds for an attack line without a weapon's enhancement or a belt.

## What the saved pages hold

The guide's three item pages were added to `PAGES` in `scripts/save-guide-pages.sh`, and the user saved them on 2026-11-09 (`guide-cache/SAVED_AT`). Read the same day:

- **`items`:** mundane weapons, armour and shields; unique items by slot (belts, boots, bracers, cloaks, glasses, gloves, headwear, necklaces, rings, rods, robes), each with its properties, cost and location; artifacts and relics; magic books; skill boosting items. For example:
  - Charisma: enhancement bonuses from +2 up to +8 (Headband of Mental Perfection +6 and +8, Razmir's Mask +8, Darkness Caress +8: Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma; Crushing Presence +6: Charisma);
  - inherent bonuses: the manuals and tomes at +2 per ability, and items giving +1, +2 or +4 to all ability scores.
- **A "Caster Level and Spell DC Boosting Items" section** in `items`, two tables of item, bonus, circumstance and location:
  - CL boosts (Sin Mage's Staff +3, Robe of the Seven Sins +3, Phantasmal Guide +3 with illusion, ...);
  - DC boosts, about 30 of them. Examples: Heartstone of the Night Hag Queen +3; Rod of Power Source +4 with conjuration; Cloak of the Ancient Herald +2 with compulsion; Mindmaster's Eyes, Twisted Temptation and Ring of Chaotic Fascination +2 with enchantment; Quarterstaff of the War Mage +2; Robe of Determination +2 against Fortitude.
- **`items-inevitable-excess` and `items-lord-of-nothing`:** the DLC items, by the same slots (Flawless Headband of Mental Perfection +8 again, Headband of Confidence +4).

## Options

- **A. Nothing:** the numbers stay without gear; the agent adds gear in words.
- **B. An "assumed" overlay on the numbers only:** `sheet` and `compare` take an optional `assume` argument (`{"cha": 6, "inherent_cha": 5, "weapon_enhancement": 5}`), applied to the attack and caster lines and shown beside the unassisted numbers. It's not part of the Build or its link and never touches a prerequisite.
- **C. Gear in the Build:** slots and items in the link. Against the map's scope rule; not recommended.

## Open questions (with recommendations)

1. **Which option?** *Recommend:* B. It keeps the scope rule for legality and lets the numbers answer "how high at the end of the game", and the agent says what it assumed.
2. **Which assumptions?** *Recommend:* a fixed list of generic bonuses (enhancement to each ability score, inherent to each, weapon enhancement, a resistance bonus to saves), with the agent supplying the numbers, so no item catalogue is needed. Each generic bonus's highest value in the guide (+8 enhancement to Charisma, for one) is given in the tool's description, from the saved pages, so the agent assumes what the game has.
3. **Named DC and CL items?** The DC and CL tables are small (about 30 and 10 rows) and their bonuses are conditional (a school, a subschool, a save), like [119](119-caster-line.md)'s sources. *Recommend:* `assume` takes items from those two tables by name, and the caster line applies each where its circumstance matches; no other named items.
4. **The web app too?** *Recommend:* not yet; the MCP server first, where the run showed the need.
5. **The map's Out of scope entry:** today it reads "Gear and companions: not part of a character's build choices", including gear bonuses toward prerequisites. *Recommend:* whichever option is taken, the answer rewrites that entry so the map says what's now covered. With B: gear stays out of the Build, its link and every prerequisite, and assumed bonuses on the computed numbers are in scope (this ticket); companions stay out. With C: gear leaves Out of scope for Notes. With A: the entry stays and names this ticket as the decision.

Settled while building (the smallest choice consistent with the map's Notes, taken while the user slept, 2026-11-09):

6. **Where the assumed numbers go:** at the Build's last level only (the ticket's question is "how high at the end of the game"), as `assumed` beside the unassisted numbers: `sheet` gives `assumed: {assumes, scores, attack?, caster?, totals}`, `compare` each summary's `assumed: {assumes, scores, attack?, caster?}` (compare shows no totals). Every part an assumption adds is named "… (assumed)" (`Weapon enhancement +5 (assumed)`, `Resistance +7 (assumed)`, `Rod of Power Source (assumed)`); the raised scores change the ability parts' values under their own names. `compare` with `assume` and neither `weapon` nor `caster` is refused (`invalid_arguments`), since it would show nothing. `by` and `improve` rank the unassisted numbers.
7. **`assume`'s shape:** `{enhancement?: {str|dex|con|int|wis|cha: n}, inherent?: {…}, weapon_enhancement?, resistance?, items?: [key]}`, nested rather than the ticket's flat sketch (`inherent_cha`), so each bonus type is one map keyed like the rest of the server's abilities. An unknown ability, a value outside the guide's range or an item not in `gear.ron` is `invalid_arguments` (the last lists the keys).
8. **A prerequisite never sees an assumed bonus:** the engine takes the assumed scores only for the numbers' amounts (ability modifiers, `AbilityModifier` amounts such as Divine Grace) and reads everything the character holds, every `requires` included, from the character's own state (`Engine::caster_line_with` and its kin take the scores apart from the state). A rules test (`a_prerequisite_never_sees_an_assumed_bonus`: a bonus requiring Charisma 20 stays out at 14 + 8 assumed) and a server test (the Build, its code, `todo`, levels and totals are the same with and without `assume`) pin it.
9. **Circumstances** (`rules::GearCircumstance`): `Spells(SpellScope)` reusing ticket 119's scopes (every spell, a school, a subschool, an energy type; never `Value`, which the data check refuses), and three the caster line can tell once it's told: `Save(Save)` ("vs. Will", "vs. Fortitude") and `MindAffecting`, read from two new optional `caster` inputs (`save`, `mind_affecting`; `rules::SpellTraits` gains them, read by assumed items only), and `Class(ClassKey)`, the caster line's casting class (the table's "Druid": Web Strider's row says "if the wearer is a Druid"; Blackened Rags' "Witch spells and Hexes": the witch's spells).
10. **Left out, rather than guessed** (`gear.ron`'s header and the README): Delamere's Armor (+2 CL with Plant domain spells), Nature's Agony and Voice of the Fearless (+2 DC with sonic spells: the caster line's energies are Elemental Focus's four), the Amulet of Blackened Mirror (hexes, no part of the caster line), Legacy of the Last Azlanti (+2 DC with "elemental" spells: which spells the guide means is a guess), and the Cloak of the Ancient Herald, whose own row gives the compulsion DC only after channelling energy to heal (an ability switched on in a fight). Also the Dagger of the Betrayer's +4 DC with fused spells and Blackened Rags' hexes.
11. **Each item's bonuses** are its table rows plus the caster-line bonuses its own row on the same page lists: Fiery Spell Weaver, Quarterstaff of the War Mage, Awakened Driftwood, Robe of the Seven Sins and Robe of Determination against spell resistance, Robe of Determination's +4 concentration. The items' other properties (Sin Mage's Staff's +6 Intelligence, the Headband of Reshaping's +6 Wisdom) are the agent's to assume with the generic bonuses. A caster level item raises the line's caster level (`caster_level_counts`, empty without one) and both checks with it; it doesn't change the spell levels castable.
12. **No slot rules:** each item's `slot` is the table's group heading (`rules::GearSlot`), but `assume` doesn't refuse two robes or two weapons; one item per slot and how bonuses stack are the agent's to judge (said in the description).

## Answer

Done 2026-11-09 (option B, every recommendation taken while the user slept). `sheet` and `compare` (MCP) take `assume`: an enhancement and an inherent bonus per ability score, a weapon's enhancement, a resistance bonus to saves, and items of the guide's two "Caster Level and Spell DC Boosting Items" tables by key. They give `assumed` at the last level, beside the unassisted numbers: the attack line, the caster line (each item's bonus only where its circumstance matches: a school, a subschool, an energy type, the spell's save, mind-affecting spells, the casting class) and, on `sheet`, the totals. Every part an assumption adds is labelled "(assumed)". The engine calls are `Engine::assumed_attack_line`, `assumed_caster_line` and `assumed_totals` over `rules::Assumed`. They read every prerequisite and `requires` from the character's own state, so a prerequisite never sees an assumed bonus. The items are game data: `libs/game-data/gear.ron` (`rules::GearItem`, 32 items, each citing `items`; six rows left out whose circumstance the caster line can't tell), loaded through migration `20261109091222_122-assumed-gear.sql`. The tool description gives each generic bonus's highest value on the saved item pages (+8 enhancement, inherent up to +4 to all from one item, weapon +5, resistance +7) and each item's bonus. The honesty boundary says assumed numbers are the agent's assumption on top of the planner's facts, and the attack line's, caster line's and totals' leaves-out lists say gear is left out unless assumed. Not in the web app. The Max DC run's enchantment compulsion DC goes from 34 to 48 at 9th level with Charisma +8 enhancement and +4 inherent, the Heartstone, Mindmaster's Eyes, Twisted Temptation and the Robe of the Seven Sins assumed.

The map's Out of scope entry on gear, rewritten (question 5), for the orchestrator to apply:

> - Gear and companions: not part of a character's build choices. Gear stays out of the Build, its link and every prerequisite: prerequisites check the character's own scores (point buy, race, level increases and ability bonuses from features such as Legend's +4), never a gear bonus (user, 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-01). Assumed bonuses on the computed numbers are in scope ([ticket 122](issues/122-assumed-gear-and-buffs.md), option B, recommended answer taken while the user slept, 2026-11-09): `sheet` and `compare` (MCP) take `assume` (generic ability, weapon and resistance bonuses, and the guide's caster level and spell DC items) and show the attack line, caster line and totals with it beside the unassisted numbers, labelled as the agent's assumption. Companions stay out.
