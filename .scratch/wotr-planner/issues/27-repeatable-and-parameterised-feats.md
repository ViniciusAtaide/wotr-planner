## Answer

Done 2026-11-01, uncommitted. Feats and selection options say how often they can be taken (once, a fixed number, any number, or once per value), a parameterised feat names what it's taken for (a skill, a weapon, an exotic weapon, a school, an energy type or an armour type), and each pick of it carries the value, in the link too. Prerequisites can need a feat for one value ("Weapon Focus (Dueling Sword)") or for the pick's own ("Weapon Specialization: a weapon for which you have already selected Weapon Focus"). Weapon Focus can be taken for two weapons and not twice for one, and Weapon Specialization checks the matching weapon, in the test world and on the real data.

### Rust (`rules`)

- **Parameters.** New `parameter.rs`: `ParameterKind` (`Skill`, `Weapon`, `ExoticWeapon`, `SpellSchool`, `Energy`, `ArmourType`) on `Feat.parameter`; a pick's value is a `Parameter` (`Skill(SkillKey)`, `Weapon(WeaponKey)`, `SpellSchool`, `Energy`, `ArmourType`; the last three closed enums the guide lists). Weapons are data: `GameData.weapons: Vec<Weapon { key, name, proficiency: Option<WeaponProficiency> }>` (`weapons.ron`); `ExoticWeapon` takes the weapons whose proficiency is `Exotic`. `Engine::parameter_values(kind)` lists a kind's values.
- **Repeats.** `Repeat` (`Once`, the default; `Times(n)`; `Unlimited`; `PerValue`) on `Feat.repeat` and `SelectionOption.repeat`. A pick past it is `Issue::AlreadyHeld` (once, or the same value again) or `Issue::MaxTimes { times }`.
- **Picks.** `Pick::Feat { feat, parameter }` and `Pick::SelectionOption { option, parameter }` (constructors `Pick::feat`, `feat_for`, `option`, `option_for`; `Pick::parameter()`). A pick must name a value of its feat's kind and only then: `Issue::ParameterMissing`, `Issue::WrongParameter` (a skill for Weapon Focus, a martial weapon for Exotic Weapon Proficiency, any value for Toughness), and an unknown skill or weapon is `UnknownKey` like any key.
- **Options with a chosen feat.** `SelectionOption.chosen_feat`: a parameterised feat the option grants for its pick's value (the rogue talents Weapon Focus and Skill Focus, "the feat of the same name"); the pick names the value, and one already held for that value is `AlreadyHeld`.
- **Prerequisites.** `Prerequisite::FeatFor { feat, value }` and `FeatForSameValue(feat)`; `Feat(feat)` is the feat in any value. `CharacterState::check_for(prerequisite, value)` checks for a pick's value (`check` is `check_for(.., None)`, where "the same value" fails).
- **State.** `CharacterState.feats` and `selection_options` are now counts (picks and grants holding each: Extra Hex taken twice is 2), and `feat_values` holds each parameterised feat's values with their counts, so a Path switch dropping one grant keeps a value a pick still holds; helpers `feat_count`, `has_feat_for`, `option_count`.
- **Grants (ticket 26's open item 5).** A grant with no pick (a row's `feats`, an option's `feats`) holds a parameterised feat for **every value**: All-skilled ("proficient with all types of weapons and armour") holds Exotic Weapon Proficiency in all 16 exotic weapons. A bonus feat of the player's choosing is a **one-feat bonus list** instead: the pick names the value.
- **Picker.** `Engine::options` lists a parameterised feat (and an option with a chosen feat) once per value, each with its own status; would-break works unchanged (refocusing Weapon Focus breaks the Specialization in its old weapon).
- **Data check.** `PerValueWithoutParameter` (also any option `PerValue`), `TimesOutOfRange` (`Times` below 2), `ChosenFeatNotParameterised`, `ValueOfWrongKind(feat)` (a value the feat isn't taken for, a non-exotic weapon for `ExoticWeapon`, "the same value" across kinds; a weapon is a weapon for `Weapon` and `ExoticWeapon` alike), `SameValueWithoutValue` ("the same value" where the pick takes none: a class, an archetype, an unparameterised feat, an option without a chosen feat). `FeatListTooShort` allows a list of one parameterised feat.
- **Link.** The `Build` type changed, so the frozen v1 vector is regenerated (no link has shipped; no v2): the v1 Build now holds Weapon Focus (Longsword), and the layout test shows the pick's trailing `parameter: None` byte.
- Tests first, in the test world (new `parameters.rs`, 14, plus 3 check tests): the world gains 5 weapons (simple, martial, two exotic, unarmed strike), Weapon Focus and Specialization, Aldori Dueling Mastery, Exotic Weapon Proficiency, Skill Focus per skill, Acrobatic Spellcaster, Spell Focus once, Augment Summoning, Extra Rogue Talent ×3, Extra Ki (any), the rogue talents Weapon Focus (chosen) and Resiliency ×2, a rogue Skill Focus bonus list at 3, and a trick granting Exotic Weapon Proficiency. ### Open questions (for the user)

1. **Which parameterised feats repeat?** The guide says so only for Weapon Focus ("a different weapon each time"); Skill Focus repeats by your decision R4. Greater Weapon Focus, Weapon Specialization, Greater Weapon Specialization, Improved Critical, Exotic Weapon Proficiency, Spell Focus, Greater Spell Focus, Elemental Focus, Greater Elemental Focus and Armor Focus are taken once, as the guide's silence reads elsewhere. My recommendation: once per value for all of them (as in the game; "for which you have already selected Spell Focus" reads as one per school). It's one line each in `feats.ron`.
2. **The weapon list.** Taken from the guide's three proficiency lists, plus Sawtooth Saber (Weapon Finesse's list says exotic, but Exotic Weapon Proficiency doesn't list it, so it can't be taken for it) and Weapon Focus's "unarmed strike, natural weapons, touch attacks and rays" as four values. Not settled by the guide: natural weapons as one value or per attack (the guide also says "Weapon Focus - Claw" and "Weapon Focus and Improved Critical with both Claw and Bite"); touch attacks and rays as one value or two; Kinetic Blast ("Weapon Focus (Kinetic Blast) will not") and bombs ("benefit from feats like Weapon Focus") left out. Improved Critical ("Select one weapon type") reads weapon type as weapon and takes the same list, extras included; weapon groups (the fighter's Weapon Training) are a different thing no feat here uses.
3. **Slashing Grace** needs "Weapon Focus (One-Handed Slashing)", and the guide doesn't list which weapons those are (only that longswords and scimitars count and the Elven Curve Blade doesn't), so it stays Weapon Focus in any weapon. Fencing Grace's "one-handed piercing" is listed and is modelled.
4. **Armor Focus** needs "Armor Proficiency (Light. Medium or Heavy)": still any of the three, not the one matching the armour type chosen (a prerequisite can't depend on the pick's value except through the same feat).
5. **Mythic feats that pick a value** aren't parameterised (the ticket names feats and options): Improved Critical (Mythic) ("your chosen weapon"), Spell Focus (Mythic) and Skill Focus (Mythic) ("Select a school/skill in which you have taken ..."), Expanded Arsenal and School Mastery ("Select one school"); nor the Trickster's Improved Improved Critical ("the selected weapon"). Their prerequisites stay "(any)", which is what the guide's prerequisite lines say.
6. **Grants still stored as features** that are really parameterised feats: the Zen Archer's Weapon Focus and Specialization ("Shortbows or Longbows", "the same weapon type"), the Mantis Zealot's Weapon Focus (Sawtooth Saber) to Greater Weapon Specialization, the Sword Saint's Weapon Focus ("his chosen weapon"), the shaman's Weapon Specialization and Greater Weapon Focus, the half-elf's Adaptability, the Chirurgeon's and Enlightened Philosopher's Skill Focus, the oracle's Weapon Focus, Improved Critical and Greater Weapon Focus. A fixed value needs a new shape (a granted feat with a value) and a restricted choice a list limited to some values; a follow-up if you want them.
7. **Level-1 order for a warpriest.** The Weapon Focus grant is now a class slot, checked after the race slot, so a human warpriest's level-1 bonus feat can't take Dazzling Display on it (it could when the feat was granted). Accept it, or check class slots before the race slot (a rules change for every class).
8. **The slayer's combat style feats** ("three times in total ... 2 or 4, 6 or 8 and 10 or greater") are a cap over a group of options at set levels, not a repeat; still not offered (report item kept).
9. Spell Specialization picks a spell, and spells are out of scope: it stays "Spell Focus" in any school.
