# A blueprint GUID on every game-data entry

Type: build
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 236
Model: Opus to build: the mapping needs judgement where names don't match.
Map: [WotR Build Planner](../map.md)

## Problem

Decided in [234](234-game-blueprints-as-a-source.md) (user, 2026-11-14): each entry in `libs/game-data/` names its blueprint by GUID. That makes the planner's data a mapped subset of the game's graph, which the game check ([238](238-the-game-check.md)) walks side by side.
- The user's question that led here: "aren't GUID's reference for you to search and create a tree of knowledge?"
- Blueprints refer to each other only by GUID. Names are not unique: "Mobility" is both an encyclopedia page and the feat.

## Change

- **The field.** An optional `blueprint` field (a 32-hex-digit GUID) on every kind of entry with a blueprint behind it: feats, classes, archetypes, prestige classes, features, selections, selection options, mythic paths and abilities, races and subraces, backgrounds, deities, items and spells.
  - **What it isn't.** The planner's keys stay as they are, since links depend on them. There is no per-fact source field (user: the recommendation): an exception, such as one of the user's facts or a code citation, goes in the data's note.
- **`GameData::check`.** It checks the format. It refuses one GUID on two entries of the same kind, unless a list of known sharings allows it: a parameterised feat's one entry, or a feature the planner splits, such as the Invulnerable Rager's Damage Reduction.
- **The mapping**, by a script kept outside the repo (as decided, no game data is committed) or by the game check's own code once [238](238-the-game-check.md) exists:
  - match each entry to its blueprint by type and name;
  - follow the GUID references from the class progressions and the feat selections (`BasicFeatSelection` and the class bonus-feat selections) to settle duplicates;
  - review the leftovers by hand.

  Entries the planner made up, with no blueprint, are left empty and listed in the ticket's Answer.

Data only, so no number or pick moves.

## Answer

**Built** (2026-11-15). Every entry with one game blueprint behind it names its GUID: 4,299 entries; 196 are left empty, listed below with their reasons. Data only: no number or pick moves, and nothing reads the field yet (ticket 238 will).

- **The field:** `BlueprintGuid` (`libs/rules/src/keys.rs`, a transparent string beside the key newtypes), and `#[serde(default, skip_serializing)] blueprint: Option<BlueprintGuid>` right after `key` on the 18 kinds: `Race`, `Subrace`, `Weapon`, `Feat`, `FeatList`, `Class` (base and prestige), `Archetype`, `Feature`, `FeatureSelection`, `SelectionOption`, `MythicAbility`, `MythicFeat`, `MythicPath`, `Background`, `Deity`, `GearItem`, `Spell`, `SpellList`. Not `Skill`: the game's skills are a stat, not a blueprint. `GameData::blueprints` lists every entry of those kinds with what it names.
- **`GameData::check`:** `MalformedBlueprint` for anything but 32 lowercase hex digits (the game's own spelling: no dashes, no braces, no capitals).
- **The mapping:** the research's script (outside the repo, at a scratch path: `index.py`, `entries.py`, `match2.py`, `outputs.py`, `apply.py`), then a hand review, kept with it as `overrides.tsv` (477 rows: kind, key, blueprint or empty, reason) and applied by `merge.py`; `verify.py` checked that the data files differ from before only by the 4,299 `blueprint` fields, each the reviewed mapping's.

**Decisions:**
- The main session, 2026-11-14: the field's type and place; the format check; one entry the game makes of several blueprints names the primary, the others in the data note; review every unconfirmed mapping by hand before writing, and leave empty what can't be settled.
- **Scope change, the user's direction via the main session, 2026-11-15:** no sharing check. `blueprint-sharings.ron`, `SharedBlueprint` and `UnusedBlueprintSharing` were dropped before they were built: any number of entries may hold one GUID for now. The rank and context splits below are the input of the ticket drafted at `.scratch/wotr-planner/issues/ranks-and-filtered-pools.md` (number to come); the copies feed a later design ticket on laying the data out by blueprint.
- Ours: structure wins over names. A feature or selection is confirmed by its granter's level entries, an option by its selection's option list; a match by name alone was taken only for kinds with unique names (feats, spells, items, backgrounds, deities). Where the game keeps an option as an ability of one feature (judgments, Geomancy terrains, Hell's decrees and seals, the Imitator's stances, Know Your Enemy's stances), the option is left empty rather than pointed at an ability.
- Ours, inferences: a mythic summon tier is its rank (the tiers with no name: `AeonSummonFeatureTier7` and `9` are the gold and adamantine golems, `AngelSummonFeatureTier8`, `9` and `AngelSummonTier10` the four astral devas and the spirit guardian(s)); the Razmiri mask's eighth step is `RazmiryInfiltratorMaskFeature9` (the planner says 8th: a difference for 238); Intense Spells is carried by `SpecializationSchoolEvocation` and Summoner's Charm by `SpecializationSchoolConjuration`; the nature mystery's companion is `OracleRevelationBondedMount`; Target Assassination is `AssassinDeathAttack`; Summon Monster 0 (`wand-of-summon-monster-zero`) is `SummonSpecialSpell`.

**The review.** Of the research's ~480 unconfirmed mappings (482 rows), 110 changed and 36 were emptied; 336 held. Of its 336 leftovers, 180 were settled and 156 stay empty (the 64 placeholders, 11 skills and 81 others below).
