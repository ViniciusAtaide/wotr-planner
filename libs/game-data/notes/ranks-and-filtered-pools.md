# Ranked features and prerequisite-filtered pools (the splits ticket)

The planner had cut some of the game's objects into several entries: one feature per step of a feature the game grants again, a revelation pool per mystery. Each group is now one entry holding its blueprint. Game sources: BubblePrintsData `2.7.0x_0`, WrathDecompiled `6ce4a6b`.

## What the data says now

- **Ranks.** A feature the game grants again gains a rank (`FeatureCollection.PrepareFactForAttach`, `Feature.AddRank`); the planner's grant count is that rank, with no cap at the blueprint's `Ranks` (decision 3).
  - `assassin/poison-use-saves-required` (`AssassinCreatePoisonIncreasedSavingThrows`, `0cbb2201a65cb374fb4d73d6c9830b01`) at assassin 5 and 9, replacing `-two-saves-required` and `-three-saves-required`: `AssassinProgression` names it at both levels, and the poison's buffs read its rank.
  - `druid/feyspeaker/fey-magic` (`FeyspeakerMagicSelection`, `237efc6a131c8174bbd466ed239f21fc`) at 4, 6, …, 18, replacing `fey-magic-i` to `-viii`. Named "Fey Magic (Feyspeaker)" beside the sorcerer's Fey bloodline's "Fey Magic". The spell each rank learns isn't modelled (decision 9).
  - `wizard/school/transmuter/physical-enhancement-2` to `-4` are gone: `TransmutationPhysicalEnhancementFeature` is granted once and its +1 to +5 scales by wizard level (`BuffScaling.ScaleBuff`), uncomputed as before. `-5` is now `physical-enhancement-capstone`, `TransmutationPhysicalEnhancementCapstone` (`6aa7d3496cd68e643adcd439a7306caa`), the transmutation progression's level 20: two abilities at once.
- **The oracle's curse is a progression.** Each `oracle/oracles-curse/<curse>` option has rows at curse levels 1, 5, 10 and 15 naming its stage features (`<Curse>CurseFeatureLevel<N>`, keys `oracle/oracles-curse/<curse>/level-<N>`), plus Blackened's `weapon-penalty` (`BlackenedCurseAttackRollFeature`) at 1 and Powerless Prophecy's own copies of Uncanny Dodge (`UncannyDodgeChecker`) at 1 and Improved Uncanny Dodge (`ImprovedUncannyDodge`) at 10 in place of its level-10 stage. 38 stage features, each with its blueprint and a summary in our own words from its components. Demonic's and Hellbound's first rows make Persuasion a class skill (`AddClassSkill`).
  - The curse level is the game's `BlueprintProgression.CalcLevel`: `option_levels: ["oracle", "witch"]`, `option_level_archetypes: ["witch/stigmatized-witch"]` (a class that has a listed archetype counts only taken with it, so a plain witch's levels don't count at all) and `half_other_classes: true` (`ForAllOtherClasses` with `AlternateProgressionType: Div2`).
  - The Stigmatized Witch (`AccursedWitchArchetype`) gets the curse slot, `witch/stigmatized-witch/curse` (`OracleCurseSelection`), in place of its placeholder feature; its key sorts after `witch/hex` so the hex keeps slot index 0.
  - The Possessed Oracle's curse is `oracle/possessed-oracle/curse`, a selection whose one option, Powerless Prophecy, is granted with no pick and advances as the class's curse (`PossessedOracleArchetype` adds `PowerlessProphecyCurseProgression` at 1). It names that progression's GUID, `88c89ab44a36a67438347e257f70c8fe`, as its former feature did: the game has no selection there (an inference of ours that the progression is the entry's object).
  - The Dual-Cursed Oracle's second curse is `oracle/second-curse` (`SecondCurseSelection`) and the Living Deity's `arcanist/magic-deceiver/ways-of-the-deceiver/living-deity/curse` (`MysteryGiftFeatureCurseSelection`): they share the nine curses and never advance (`skipped_levels: [5, 10, 15]`), as the game's `<Curse>CurseNoProgression` options have a level-1 entry only. Their keys keep each slot's index in its row, so links keep their picks. Two NoProgression level-1 features differ from the progression's (Blackened's penalty stays -4, Wolf-Scarred Face's bite stays 1d4); the planner holds the progression's and its summaries say the difference.
  - The 27 placeholders `oracle/curse-2` to `-4` and the archetypes' copies are gone, with the archetype rows they left equal to the class's. `bonuses.ron`'s Powerless Prophecy +4 initiative and Pranked -4 now hang on their stages (`level-5`, `level-1`), so they follow the curse level, not oracle 5.
- **One revelation selection, filtered by the mystery.** The nine `oracle/revelations/<mystery>` pools merged into `oracle/revelations` (`OracleRevelationSelection`, `60008a10ad7ad6543b1f63016741a5d2`, 62 options), which the oracle's rows now name (`oracle/revelation` is gone). `filtered_by: "oracle/mystery"`: each revelation's prerequisite names its mystery (the game's `PrerequisiteFeaturesFromList`), and the picker leaves out the ones whose mystery isn't held (decision 4; the game shows them greyed). Every option key stays.
  - Combat Healer is one option, `oracle/revelations/battle/combat-healer`, admitted by Battle or Life; `oracle/revelations/life/combat-healer-life` is its alias.
  - Fortune and Misfortune moved in as `oracle/revelations/fortune` and `/misfortune`, each needing the Dual-Cursed Oracle; their old keys are aliases. A plain oracle's picker lists them locked.
  - The Dual-Cursed Oracle's `oracle/dual-cursed-oracle/revelation` and the Living Deity's `arcanist/magic-deceiver/revelation` keep their keys (their slots' indexes) and share the 62 options, filtered by `oracle/mystery` and `arcanist/magic-deceiver/mystery` (decision 5: copies until the layout ticket's `options_from`).
  - `aliases` on an option are read only by `LinkKeys`; the no-shared-link-hash test counts them.

## Decisions

- The main session, from the research's recommendations under the user's "do everything as planned" (2026-11-15): items 1 to 9 of the ticket's Answer (keys and aliases, "rank n" on the sheet, the rank as the grant count, a stored `filtered_by`, the Living Deity's copy, stage summaries in our own words, Physical Enhancement, the curse slots, what is out of scope).
- Ours: `filtered_by` sits on the selection and names the selection whose options admit (the admitting options are those its prerequisite names), so nothing is stored twice; the curse's level fields sit on each selection the curse is held through (the oracle's, the Stigmatized Witch's, the Possessed Oracle's), as the game's are on each progression; Fortune keeps no level: the game's `PrerequisiteArchetypeLevel` asks for Dual-Cursed level 5, and 25 corpus links that open today pick it before then, so it is a difference for the game check (ticket 238) rather than a change here.

## Not modelled

The spell each Fey Magic rank learns; the Elemental Rampager's element progressions; Enchanting Smile's class-level number; Strength of the Abyss's `AddLevel2` (238). Physical Enhancement's bonus, the curses' numbers other than initiative.
