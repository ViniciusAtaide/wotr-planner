# Ranked features and prerequisite-filtered pools (the splits ticket)

The planner had cut some of the game's objects into several entries: one feature per step of a feature the game grants again, a revelation pool per mystery. Each group is now one entry holding its blueprint. Game sources: BubblePrintsData `2.7.0x_0`, WrathDecompiled `6ce4a6b`.

## What the data says now

- **Feature ranks.** A feature the game grants again gains a rank (`FeatureCollection.PrepareFactForAttach`), up to the blueprint's `Ranks` (`Feature.AddRank` stops there). The planner's feature rank is the grant count, uncapped (decision 3): within the cap it is the game's rank (the Assassin's poison saves, `Ranks` 5: rank 2 at assassin 9); past it the planner counts on where the game stays (Fey Magic's selection has `Ranks` 1).
  - `assassin/poison-use-saves-required` (`AssassinCreatePoisonIncreasedSavingThrows`, `0cbb2201a65cb374fb4d73d6c9830b01`) at assassin 5 and 9, replacing `-two-saves-required` and `-three-saves-required`: `AssassinProgression` names it at both levels, and the poison's buffs read its rank.
  - `druid/feyspeaker/fey-magic` (`FeyspeakerMagicSelection`, `237efc6a131c8174bbd466ed239f21fc`) at 4, 6, …, 18, replacing `fey-magic-i` to `-viii`. Named "Fey Magic (Feyspeaker)" beside the sorcerer's Fey bloodline's "Fey Magic". The spell each rank learns isn't modelled (decision 9).
  - `wizard/school/transmuter/physical-enhancement-2` to `-4` are gone: `TransmutationPhysicalEnhancementFeature` is granted once and its +1 to +5 scales by wizard level (`BuffScaling.ScaleBuff`), uncomputed as before. `-5` is now `physical-enhancement-capstone`, `TransmutationPhysicalEnhancementCapstone` (`6aa7d3496cd68e643adcd439a7306caa`), the transmutation progression's level 20: two abilities at once.
- **The oracle's curse is the curse's own progression** (the game's `BlueprintProgression`). Each `oracle/oracles-curse/<curse>` option has rows at curse levels 1, 5, 10 and 15 naming its stage features (`<Curse>CurseFeatureLevel<N>`, keys `oracle/oracles-curse/<curse>/level-<N>`), plus Blackened's `weapon-penalty` (`BlackenedCurseAttackRollFeature`) at 1 and Powerless Prophecy's own copies of Uncanny Dodge (`UncannyDodgeChecker`) at 1 and Improved Uncanny Dodge (`ImprovedUncannyDodge`) at 10 in place of its level-10 stage. 38 stage features, each with its blueprint and a summary in our own words from its components. Demonic's and Hellbound's first rows make Persuasion a class skill (`AddClassSkill`).
  - The curse level is the game's `BlueprintProgression.CalcLevel`: `option_levels: ["oracle", "witch"]`, `option_level_archetypes: ["witch/stigmatized-witch"]` (a class that has a listed archetype counts only taken with it, so a plain witch's levels don't count at all) and `half_other_classes: true` (`ForAllOtherClasses` with `AlternateProgressionType: Div2`). The other classes include the mythic ones: `CalcLevel` sums every entry of `unit.Progression.Classes` it doesn't list, and those hold the mythic classes, whose levels make the Mythic level (`UnitProgressionData.SetupLevelsIfNecessary`); so half the Mythic rank counts, and a rank taken re-evaluates the curse level, as every level-up recomputes each progression's (`ApplyClassMechanics.ApplyProgressions`). A pure oracle 9 at MR 3 holds the level-10 stage.
  - The Stigmatized Witch (`AccursedWitchArchetype`) gets the curse slot, `witch/stigmatized-witch/curse` (`OracleCurseSelection`), in place of its placeholder feature; its key sorts after `witch/hex` so the hex keeps slot index 0.
  - The Possessed Oracle's curse is `oracle/possessed-oracle/curse`, a selection whose one option, Powerless Prophecy, is granted with no pick and advances as the class's curse (`PossessedOracleArchetype` `09035bb5dbb714645bb08ea7db47884c` adds `PowerlessProphecyCurseProgression` `88c89ab44a36a67438347e257f70c8fe` at 1). The selection is the planner's: the game adds the progression directly, with no selection, so it names no blueprint. The archetype and the option (`oracle/oracles-curse/powerless-prophecy`) hold the citations.
  - The Dual-Cursed Oracle's second curse is `oracle/second-curse` (`SecondCurseSelection`) and the Living Deity's `arcanist/magic-deceiver/ways-of-the-deceiver/living-deity/curse` (`MysteryGiftFeatureCurseSelection`): they share the nine curses and never advance (`skipped_levels: [5, 10, 15]`), as the game's `<Curse>CurseNoProgression` options have a level-1 entry only. Their keys keep each slot's index in its row, so links keep their picks. Two NoProgression level-1 features differ from the progression's: `BlackenedCurseAttackRollFeatureNoProgression` (`7e3e8136fbf845d3b3a27f92b3d84827`) grants the -4 (`BlackenedCurseAttackRollLevel1`) from level 1 with no -2 at 10, and `WolfScarredFaceCurseFeatureLevel1NoProgression` (`3719895b1519447b8bb65428351507ba`) the 1d4 bite from level 1 with no end at 5 (`AddFeatureOnClassLevel`, where the progression's have `BeforeThisLevel` and a later swap). The planner holds the progression's features, and the two selections' summaries say the difference.
  - The 27 placeholders `oracle/curse-2` to `-4` and the archetypes' copies are gone, with the archetype rows they left equal to the class's. `bonuses.ron`'s Powerless Prophecy +4 initiative and Pranked -4 now hang on their stages (`level-5`, `level-1`), so they follow the curse level, not oracle 5.
- **One revelation selection, filtered by the mystery.** The nine `oracle/revelations/<mystery>` pools merged into `oracle/revelations` (`OracleRevelationSelection`, `60008a10ad7ad6543b1f63016741a5d2`, 62 options), which the oracle's rows now name (`oracle/revelation` is gone). `filtered_by: "oracle/mystery"`: each revelation's prerequisite names its mystery (the game's `PrerequisiteFeaturesFromList`), and the picker leaves out the ones whose prerequisite can't be met without a mystery not held (decision 4; the game shows them greyed). A mystery named under a `Not`, or beside another way in, never hides an option. Every option key stays.
  - Combat Healer is one option, `oracle/revelations/battle/combat-healer`, admitted by Battle or Life; `oracle/revelations/life/combat-healer-life` is its alias.
  - Fortune and Misfortune moved in as `oracle/revelations/fortune` and `/misfortune`, each needing the Dual-Cursed Oracle, Fortune from oracle 5 (their `PrerequisiteArchetypeLevel`: `FortuneRevelationFeature` `16935c4660ee4c7da976b5f6cafb43dd` level 5, `MisfortuneRevelationFeature` `8121fe70a7974970a8a560bd2267cc24` level 1); their old keys are aliases. A plain oracle's picker lists them locked.
  - The Dual-Cursed Oracle's `oracle/dual-cursed-oracle/revelation` and the Living Deity's `arcanist/magic-deceiver/revelation` keep their keys (their slots' indexes) and share the 62 options, filtered by `oracle/mystery` and `arcanist/magic-deceiver/mystery` (decision 5: copies until the layout ticket's `options_from`).
  - `aliases` on an option are read only by `LinkKeys`; the no-shared-link-hash test counts them.

## Decisions

- The main session, from the research's recommendations under the user's "do everything as planned" (2026-11-15): items 1 to 9 of the ticket's Answer (keys and aliases, "rank n" on the sheet, the rank as the grant count, a stored `filtered_by`, the Living Deity's copy, stage summaries in our own words, Physical Enhancement, the curse slots, what is out of scope).
- Ours: `filtered_by` sits on the selection and names the selection whose options admit (the admitting options are those its prerequisite names), so nothing is stored twice; the curse's level fields sit on each selection the curse is held through (the oracle's, the Stigmatized Witch's, the Possessed Oracle's), as the game's are on each progression. The main session, at review (2026-11-15): Fortune gets the game's level 5 (ticket 234: the game wins; the 25 corpus links that took it at oracle 1 or 3 were the engine's own earlier output), and the Mythic rank counts in a curse's level.

## Not modelled
